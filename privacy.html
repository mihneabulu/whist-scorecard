Privacy Policy

Whist Scorecard · Effective 27 September 2026

The short version

Whist Scorecard collects nothing. Your games never leave your device.

What the app stores

The players' names and the games you record — bids, tricks and scores — are saved on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not use the network at all.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or cloud sync.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party code that collects data.

Backups and deleting your data

Your games are part of your device's normal backup (iCloud or computer backup), under your control. Deleting the app deletes its games. You can also delete a single game from the list, or a player from Players.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes — for example, if sync is added — this page will be updated before the app update that does it, and the date above will change.
