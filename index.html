Common questions

Why can't the dealer press some numbers? The dealer bids last and may not make the bids add up to the number of cards, so that bid is shown in red and can't be chosen.

How is a hand scored? Take exactly the tricks you bid and you score your bid plus 5. Miss, and you lose the difference.

Can I fix a number after the game is over? Open the game and tap Edit. It goes back into play; finish it again when you're done.

Are my games backed up? They are kept on the device only, and included in your device's normal backup. Deleting the app deletes them.
